# Gallery scope

This unofficial static gallery presents the original v0.1.0 selection: 92 GLB variants covering 87 source IDs, including 5 low-detail variants, and 102 PNG textures. Source IDs are preserved rather than assigning unverified commercial character names.

## Previews and downloads

Every thumbnail is a presentation render of its corresponding included GLB, generated with the pinned Three.js renderer. Previews apply a display-only 90-degree X rotation and scale/centering to present these exports upright; the downloaded files retain their original transforms and bytes. The gallery uses lazy-loaded WebP images in the grid and creates only one active WebGL model inspector. Closing it releases the renderer and model resources. Search, variant filters, sorting, keyboard controls, touch orbit/zoom, and direct GLB/PNG download links work without an account.

The original model, texture, catalog, rights, validation, and checksum files are unchanged. SHA256SUMS.txt covers the original package files other than itself; it does not cover later gallery source, dependencies, or thumbnails. The original v0.1.0 structural audit predated these gallery renders. The renders are not a source-game appearance comparison or an additional certification of visual fidelity, units, materials, topology, complete rigs, or animation correctness. Some small exports may be partial objects.

## Experimental animation previews

Playback is optional and always plays once. Most models have no clips. A01's 102 clips are accessory/reference clips, not authenticated body animations. A07's idle and walk_forward are measured clips, without a guarantee of seamless looping or gameplay correctness. The separate A07 add-on is not included in this gallery or release.
