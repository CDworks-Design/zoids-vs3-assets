12 standard model IDs · 205 take records. Selected Blender-compatible FBX packages with embedded textures. The full roster is not finished; take records are not all unique moving animations.

These are downloads, not browser-playable FBX previews. The existing GLB gallery is unchanged. Blender 4.3.2 was checked; Unreal behavior, native-game fidelity, arbitrary-time interpolation and seamless loops are not certified.
