Reading this inventory

Source provenance

“Image-identical” means the extractor file matched the compared translated disc image. “Local generated” identifies a file produced locally. Neither label establishes original retail provenance or a resource's in-game purpose.

Conversion availability

“Verified locally” means an export matches its recorded hash. Header and structure checks have a bounded scope; they do not establish visual fidelity or runtime behavior. A download appears only for an explicitly published model.

Names and maps

Labels preserve source IDs. Weapon resource joins are evidence links, not verified commercial weapon names. Stage records can be separate objects or backdrops rather than complete assembled maps.

Independent asset research · Rights and reuse · Inventory documentation
